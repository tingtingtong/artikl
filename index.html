Client dump → books pack → GST review. That’s the MVP. We win that before we add anything else.

01

Dump

Clients send bank statements, invoices, Form 16s, and GSTR JSONs on WhatsApp, email, or upload. Artikl classifies every file.

02

Books pack

Extracted lines land in Excel and Tally-ready CSV — party, GSTIN, tax breakup, confidence scores, and an exceptions list for juniors.

03

GST review

GSTR-1 vs sales, 2B vs purchases, 1 vs 3B — with ₹ impact ranked. You mark fix / ignore / escalate. Then you sign off.

Hard rule: Artikl never auto-files to GSTN or the income-tax portal. AI prepares. The CA decides.
